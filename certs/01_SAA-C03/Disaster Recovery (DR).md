
---
### 🧩 Definition  
A set of policies, tools, and procedures that enable the **recovery or continuation** of vital technology infrastructure and systems after a **natural** or **human-induced** disaster.

---

### 🎯 Goal  
- Plan **how** we recover when **HA** and **FT** fails

---
### How Does It Work?

#### 🧠 Pre-Planning  
- **Why?**  
  - To ensure decisions aren’t made under stress or pressure.  
- **Example:**  
  - Taking regular backups and storing them at a separate site.

#### 🔁 DR Process  
- A predefined set of actions to follow when a disaster occurs.  
- **Key Questions:**  
  - Where are the backups stored?  
  - How to access them?  
  - How to restore them on a standby location?

#### 🔄 Periodic DR Testing  
- Run simulated disaster scenarios to ensure your DR plan is effective.  
- Helps validate:  
  - Recovery time  
  - Team preparedness  
  - Backup validity
