General Purpose SSD : GP2 
- Can be as small as 1GB and as big as 16TB
- An IO credit is 16KB
- An IOPS is 1 IO in 1 second 
- 100 min IO Credit per second and beyond 3 IO per second per GB of vol size
- Burst up to 3,000 IOPS
- IO initial credit capacity 5.4 millions
- Volumes over 1TB have baseline above the birst 
- Good for : 
	- Boot volumes 
	- low-latency interactive apps 
General Purpose GP3
- No credit system 
- GP3 is cheaper than GP2 (~20%)
- Standard 3k IOPS & 125Mbps
- Extra cost for up to 16k IOPS & 1k MBPs
- 4x faster throughput than GP2
